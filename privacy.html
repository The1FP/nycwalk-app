Privacy Policy

NYC Walk · Effective October 15, 2026

NYC Walk is a free walking-tour app for New York City, paid for by ads. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. We don't sell your data.

Your location

The app uses your location while a walk is running, and while the map is open, to announce neighborhoods and tell you about nearby places. Most of this happens on your iPhone. Two services outside the app receive your location to make it work:

  • Wikipedia (run by the Wikimedia Foundation). The app sends your current position to Wikipedia to find articles about places near you. It is sent without your name or any account. See the Wikimedia privacy policy.
  • Apple Maps. The map, walking directions and Look Around street imagery come from Apple. See Apple's privacy policy.

We never receive or store your location ourselves. You can turn off location access at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Apps, NYC Walk, Location.

Camera

The AR view uses your camera to float signs over the places around you. The camera image is processed only on your iPhone. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Information stored on your iPhone

Your settings, the list of places you've heard, and any "wrong fact" reports you write are saved only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us. A report leaves your phone only if you choose to share it yourself, for example by email.

Apple Intelligence

On iPhones that support it, Apple Intelligence rewrites some Wikipedia text into spoken stories. This runs on your iPhone.

Ads

NYC Walk shows ads from Google AdMob. To choose and measure ads, Google's ad software in the app collects information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), your IP address and the approximate location it suggests, how you interact with ads, and crash and performance data. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The first time you use the app, iPhone asks whether NYC Walk may track you. If you allow it, ads can be personalized to your interests. If you don't, you still see ads, but they aren't personalized. You can change this at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security, Tracking. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. NYC Walk does not share your walking location with Google.

Children

NYC Walk is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above before the version of the app it applies to is released.
