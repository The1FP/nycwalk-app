NYC Walk

A walking tour of New York City that talks to you as you go.

Start a walk and put your phone in your pocket. NYC Walk tells you when you enter a new neighborhood and plays short stories about the landmarks you pass, with which side of the street they're on. It covers Manhattan, Brooklyn and Queens.

Help

Stories don't play, or the app can't find me. Open the iPhone Settings app, go to Apps, NYC Walk, Location, and choose "While Using the App". Make sure Precise Location is on, so stories start when you reach a place and not a few blocks away.

Stories stop when my screen is locked. Keep the walk running (don't tap Stop) and keep location set to "While Using the App". iOS shows a blue location pill while the walk runs in the background.

The voice sounds robotic. Apple offers free, more natural voices. In NYC Walk, open Settings and tap the voice guide to download one. Then pick it in the app.

A story is wrong, or a dot is in the wrong place. Tap the flag on the story to write a note, then share it with us by email.

I'm not in New York. In Settings, under "Try it from anywhere", play the demo walk to hear the app as if you were walking through the Financial District.
